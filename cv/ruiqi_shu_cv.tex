\section*{Research Interests}
AI for Earth system science; AI for Science; differentiable geophysical and ocean solvers; neural--physics hybrid modeling; spatiotemporal prediction; geophysical fluid dynamics; marine heatwave forecasting; ocean state estimation. I am particularly interested in transforming traditional atmospheric and oceanic numerical solvers into differentiable systems and coupling them deeply with neural networks. I am also developing \href{https://neural-pom.vercel.app/}{Neural-POM}, a project that combines a full differentiable ocean numerical model with machine learning.
