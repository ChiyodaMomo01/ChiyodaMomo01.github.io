\section*{Research Interests}
My research focuses on machine learning for the physical world, particularly the modeling, simulation, and prediction of dynamical systems. I am interested in differentiable numerical solvers, hybrid physics--learning models, and physically consistent world models. My goal is to connect learning from data with physical laws and numerical computation to model dynamics across spatial and temporal scales. Earth systems, including the atmosphere and oceans, provide concrete settings for this research.
